02 / THE IDEAAn invitation to make-believe.

The world is
make-believe.

Why stop
playing?

Remember when a few bright shapes could become anything? A castle. A tool. An entirely different world.

Poor Little Thing borrows that language of childhood play and gives it to the adult imagination. Familiar colors become objects of curiosity, tension, and desire.

Kink is make-believe, too. A role, a ritual, a world we choose together. These pieces explore the space where playfulness meets pain, and an ordinary object becomes something else entirely.

Cut loose. Be curious. Play a little.

Art objects for adults. Play begins with consent.
