# Poor Little Thing

## Register

brand

## Users and purpose

Adults browse an art-object catalogue on phones and computers, turn the 3D previews, and follow individual Cults3D links to buy personal-use print files. The site is a static GitHub Pages showcase, with no checkout or hosted print downloads.

## Brand personality

Bright, playful, tactile. The existing identity uses primary toy colors, warm paper, Albert Sans, supplied toy-world backdrops, and the approved red logo. The public copy describes make-believe and adult play.

## Design principles

- Preserve the existing brand, models, camera poses, artwork and purchase links.
- Organize products by recognizable object type, with visible section headings.
- Show six products initially in each section, with an independent Show more control where needed.
- Keep real product imagery and interactive turntables as the focus.

## Anti-references

The user replaced the broad All/Toys/Apparel filter with named product sections. Do not restore a filter-only catalogue or direct print downloads.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Retain semantic headings, 44-pixel controls, visible keyboard focus, slider keyboard controls, image fallbacks, reduced-motion support, and mobile layouts. Disclosure buttons must expose their expanded state and control the relevant product grid.
